======================================================================================
CSR integrand in band-relative coordinates v = (x' - centre_k) / half_k
======================================================================================
v = 0 is the located branch; |v| <= 1 is the band the quadrature covers.
"inside" is the fraction of total |integrand| magnitude at |v| <= 1, over
the sampled strip |v| <= 3. It should be close to 1 for a live branch.

  UNION coverage = fraction of |integrand| over a 40-half-width window
  that lies inside the union of the located bands. This is the number
  that matters: a low per-branch "inside" is harmless if the other band
  covers it, but low UNION coverage means real support is being missed.
